About Us

Discover the story behind KinG Watches and the vision that drives our brand.

Who We Are

KinG Watches was founded by Kenneth Vargas with a simple but powerful vision: to bring the elegance and craftsmanship of luxury timepieces to a wider audience. Inspired by the idea that style and quality shouldn’t be limited by price, he set out to create watches that feel premium without the premium cost. To bring that vision to life online, Kenneth partnered with Kinza and Gabriella to design a modern platform that showcases each watch as a piece of art. Together, they built KinG Watches not just as a store, but as a brand that represents confidence, accessibility, and timeless design.

Our Mission

We provide elegant, affordable, and high-quality watches for everyone. Our goal is to make luxury style accessible without compromising performance or durability.
